What is worth knowing next time:

- **`scenes/props.py` cannot be imported by bare name.** Every video in this repo
  has one, the deck route puts each video's `scenes/` on a FLAT `sys.path`, and
  `tests/test_slides_convention.py` builds all three films in one pytest session
  — so whichever `props` is imported first wins the name for the process and this
  film's set failed with a name error about a class sitting in its own directory.
  `tokenizer_set.py` loads it by file location under a unique module name. If a
  fourth video appears, it needs the same treatment (or a rename).
- **`FadeIn` animates UP TO a mobject's current opacity.** Anything built at
  opacity 0 fades in to 0. `StreamingBubble` builds its text that way, so beat 0
  streamed an EMPTY bubble through a whole draft; it needs `reveal()` first.
  Everything else that arrives later is simply left out of the scene graph until
  its beat rather than parked at zero.
- **An animation adds its mobject to the scene's TOP level.** Wrapping
  already-added children in a fresh `VGroup` and animating that draws them twice
  and makes the later `FadeOut` a no-op on the originals. Every clear-down here
  therefore fades the same objects the arrival added — splatted, not wrapped.
- **Pango lays a `Text` out against the RENDER's pixel width.** A 45-unit caption
  at `SHOT_WIDE` wrapped to two lines at 480p and would not have at 1080p, i.e.
  a caption whose shape depends on the profile it was reviewed in. Long wide-shot
  captions now carry explicit line breaks.
- **A wide label added before a bay filled at full opacity is never seen.** The
  QUESTION marquee was drawn underneath the chat window for the whole pull-back
  and nothing but a frame said so. `wide_labels` is added LAST.
- **`PipelineBox`'s title is sized for the pull-back and hangs outside the frame.**
  At `SHOT_IDS` it was cropped in half by the top edge, so the model box carries a
  close-up name of its own and the big one joins `wide_labels`.
- `.animate.scale` on a container `VGroup` was probed before beat 6 relied on it
  (the set shrinks into the ghost node while the camera holds). At manim 0.21 it
  does NOT dim the children; the repo's standing warning did not reproduce.
