DECISIONS WORTH KNOWING
- Scene 3 uses "tokenization" rather than "ChatGPT" in the demo sentence,
  because simple_tokenize only splits words longer than 7 characters and the
  scene needs a visible split. If the sentence changes, check the split still
  happens — `_split_pieces` returns empty otherwise and the callout silently
  disappears.
- The scene's `_callout` / `_callout_group` brace helpers are deliberately local.
  They are plausibly reusable, but only one scene needs them so far. Promote to
  lib/components/ when a second scene reaches for them.
- ChatInput.clear() was promoted into /lib during this session (with a test) —
  every chat-driven video needs a composer reset, so it passed the bar.
