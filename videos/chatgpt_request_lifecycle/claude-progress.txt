SESSION 8 — the whole video, in one pass
====================================================================

Built videos/chatgpt_request_lifecycle from nothing: a 45-second continuous
shot through the full request lifecycle, in the same house format as
chatgpt_message_journey. That film showed the model; this one shows the plant
the model sits inside. videos/chatgpt_message_journey/ was NOT touched — it is
a reviewed, shipped deliverable and rewriting it into a nine-station film would
have destroyed it. The two share the /lib vocabulary, the palette and the four
in-box station accents on purpose, so the new one reads as the same world seen
wider.

WHAT IS HERE
- scenes/lifecycle_set.py   the set: four bands, ten stations, one closed loop
- scenes/scene_lifecycle.py the choreography: eleven beats, each with its

- scenes/props.py           RequestCard, StampChip, BatchLanes — one call site

- script.md, scenes.json, feature_list.json, AGENTS.md (visual conventions

MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED
The plan's figures were checked against a constructed set rather than trusted:

  world extent      55.89 x 29.70
  wide_frame_width  57.89  (SHOT_WIDE = 58.0, drift 0.19%)
  pull-back         4.07x
  circuit length    128.45   way home 52.79

Every bay centre and extent came out exactly where the plan's table said, and
the box edges are DERIVED from llm.frame rather than hardcoded — validate()
asserts them rather than the other way round.

THE SIX ASSERTIONS IN validate()
1. the climb home clears every bay, the chat, the bot check and the box frame
2. and so do the CHORDS a comet draws along it, budgeted at
   LOOP_FPS * LOOP_RUN_TIME * (home_len / circuit_len) frames — which is why
   LOOP_RUN_TIME lives in the set and the scene imports it
3. the fan-out spur, same obstacle set
4. band separation, as four separately-worded checks: top band above the box,
   box above the stream bay, after bay clear of the bottom band, RETURN_X clear
   of both the chat and the after bay
5. the zoom budget against SHOT_WIDE, with an error that says to fix the layout
   rather than widen the constant
6. typography.audit over every wide-shot label. The two ROTATED labels (the box
   title and the return label) are audited through an unrotated copy: measure()
   compares a bounding-box height against frame height, and for a label turned
   on its side that height is the LENGTH of the words, so it would pass
   trivially and tell us nothing.

WHAT THE FRAMES CAUGHT (none of it visible to the Evaluator)
Seven real bugs, all found by rendering at the draft profile and reading the
contact sheet and full-size stills. This is the whole argument for the review
loop:

1. THE REPLY WAS LEGIBLE TEN SECONDS EARLY. ChatWindow.post -> commit ->
   _scroll_into_view -> _hide_scrolled_off calls set_opacity(1.0) on every
   message still in view, which undoes the hidden state a StreamingBubble is
   built in. So the finished sentence was readable from the moment the bubble
   was posted, and every later reveal(n) was a no-op. Fixed with an explicit
   reveal(0) after post. The reference film has the same latent problem.
2. THE QUESTION DISAPPEARED WHEN THE ANSWER LANDED. At CHAT_H=6.4 the window's
   message area is 3.29 units and the two bubbles need 3.47, so the transcript
   scrolled and hid the question — exactly as ChatWindow is documented to do.
   CHAT_H is 7.0 now. Measured, not guessed.
3. A BLACK FRAME MID-SHOT. The swing from the chat down to the after bay is 25
   units of empty world, and at W_SERVICE the camera spent about half a second
   looking at nothing. W_AFTER=20 with the frame pushed 2.0 left keeps the
   return rail in shot for the whole move.
4. THE CHAT WAS SLICED BY THE FRAME EDGE during the long travel shot, because
   the camera was framed against the return rail alone and the rail tops out 3.2
   units below the window. Framed against VGroup(rail, chat) now, at W=45.
5. THE DECODE CAPTION AND ITS METER LANDED ON TOP OF EACH OTHER. Both were
   placed under the bay independently and the 0.6-unit gap only fits one. They
   are one arranged row now, which is also why the caption is shorter.
6. THE TOKENIZER SHOWED TWO THINGS AT ONCE. The chat template's fade-out and the
   chips' entrance shared a 1.2s play and the same slot, so the middle of the
   beat was mono text showing through a row of chips. Two plays now.
7. THE PREFILL BEAT NAMED THE WRONG THING. SegmentedBar drops an inline label
   below 12% of the bar and "new tail" is 5%, so the emphasised amber sliver had
   nothing anywhere saying what it was. The caption names it now.

DELIBERATE DEPARTURES FROM THE PLAN (with reasons)
- The packet stops at a band station's DOOR, not at slot_center. The slot is
  where the CheckList and the meters go; a dot on top of a tick list reads as a
  rendering mistake. Stopping it on the incoming rail says "the request is at
  the Edge tier" just as clearly.
- The decode beat's TransformerStack is shown=2, not shown=3. This bay is 2.1
  tall where the reference film's was 2.4, and three blocks get fit-scaled to
  ~0.75, which puts the layer captions under typography.MIN_READABLE.
- The token strip is per_line=4, not 3, for the same reason: three rows is 2.3
  tall against a 1.6 slot.
- The decode meter sits UNDER the bay rather than in the slot, beside the
  caption. The slot is 4.6 x 1.6 and the stack and the lanes already have it.
- BatchLanes labels only the highlighted row. The plan floated "…" on the muted
  rows; at this size that is four more pieces of noise and the colour already
  says which row to follow.

KNOWN-WEAK, NOT HIDDEN
- lib/components/probability.py predates lib/typography.py and still sizes its
  text in absolute theme points, so an unscaled ProbabilityBar's value text
  lands just under MIN_READABLE at this film's shot widths. TheLifecycle._meter
  compensates by scaling the whole widget. That is a workaround; the real fix is
  a frame_width argument, which moves the probability_chart baseline and so
  deserves its own session. Tracked as `probability-typography`.
- ProbabilityBar.set_value replaces value_mob, so the submobject list changes.
  It was probed on a throwaway scene before being relied on: .animate.set_value
  DOES interpolate cleanly (Manim aligns the two glyph trees), and the number
  morphs rather than ticking. Over 0.9s that reads as a meter filling. The
  fallback path — animating bar.bar.stretch_to_fit_width and swapping the text
  separately — was not needed.
- At 15 fps the comet leaves a visible corner-cutting chord where the reply
  turns out of the box. It is in open space, crosses nothing, and is four times
  shorter at the 60 fps final profile. Not worth slowing the move for.
- IconTile's caption ("bot check") is sized in absolute points by /lib, so it is
  small at the pull-back. It names a minor node and the tile's icon carries the
  meaning; left alone rather than special-cased.
