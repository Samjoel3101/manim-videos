SESSION (tenth): the film taught autoregressive generation WRONG. Fixed.
================================================================================
Not a polish pass. A correctness fix to what the film ASSERTS.

THE DEFECT
  beat_pull_back ran its end-of-film runner twice around s.circuit() - the
  ENTIRE plant - and lit all ten of s.nodes on each pass, presenting each lap as
  producing more of the answer. That says every token you receive is re-scored
  at the edge, re-authenticated at the gateway and re-assembled by the
  orchestrator. It is not. Prefill runs ONCE, in a single parallel pass that
  fills the KV cache; every subsequent token is ONE decode step that takes only
  the previously generated token, attends to the cached keys and values, and
  appends its own K/V to the cache. Nothing walks back upstream per token.
  Separately: the beat revealed 3 words up front and 4 per "token" over two
  passes - 2 tokens, 8 words, against an 11-word reply. A viewer who counts,
  which is the only kind of viewer this film has, sees the arithmetic fail.

WHAT THE SET GAINED
  - rail_sample_to_cache: a DRAWN loop-back rail, sampler -> prefill/KV, up the
    box's inner right margin at LOOPBACK_X=23.25. The margin is 0.90 wide
    (frame right 23.70, bays stop at 22.80) and was empty; 23.25 is its midline,
    0.45 from each. Chevrons point UP at the cache, which the travel order gives
    for free. Routed through the KV bay rather than straight to decode because
    the sampled token's K,V are what the next step reads - that makes the cache
    the hinge of the cycle and reuses the two rails already drawn.
  - loopback_label "append K,V - next token", rotated, in the margin outside the
    frame, mirroring the box's own side title. Clamped to 92% of the box height:
    unclamped it measured 18.2 units end to end against a 12.8-tall box and hung
    across the bottom band. Centring it on the loop's own 5.4-unit span was
    tried and is worse - the scale that fits lands at 0.013 of frame height,
    a third under typography.MIN_READABLE.
  - decode_cycle(): the loop, joined from the drawn rails, both end bays entered
    and left on an L so the payload never doubles back. It CLOSES exactly

  - request_nodes / loop_nodes. `nodes` stays as an inventory with a docstring

  - LOOP_RUN_TIME -> REQUEST_LAP_RUN_TIME (1.6), new EMIT_RUN_TIME (0.40);
    validate() budgets chord clearance for the lap and for the token's flight
    home against the real numbers.

REVIEW FOLLOW-UP (same session, after the independent pacing review)

SESSION 11 — beat_orchestrator: the legend moves below the bar.

WHY
  The four category NAMES were 2.6-3.0x below typography.MIN_READABLE while
  every number cleared it — a /lib defect in stacked.py::_build_legend, fixed
  there and written up in the repo-level log. The layout change here is what
  makes the fix land in this bay.

  Widening the legend in place was not available. A readable row needs
  0.45 (swatch+PAD_SM) + 2.10 (longest name) + 0.25 + 0.73 (value column)
  = 3.53 of the slot's 4.9, which leaves the bar 0.87 — and this is the shot
  whose whole point is that "your message" is a 0.18% sliver of a bar long
  enough to see it in.

CHANGED
  - length 2.3 -> 4.9, legend_side="below", legend_width dropped (it defaults to
    the bar's length), strict_legend=True.
  - sources chips gutter PAD_MD -> PAD_SM. That row measured 5.21 against a 4.90
    slot and was what made station.fit WIDTH-bound, so it was shrinking the bar
    and its legend to suit itself. At PAD_SM it is 4.71 and the binding
    constraint is the height, which is what the layout is budgeting.
  - VGroup(sources, bar) buff PAD_SM -> PAD_XS.
